# Villages & Factions

The three playable villages and the rogue element are the game's core factions. Village membership drives friend/foe logic, skirmish teams, territory wars, and which quests an NPC will offer you.

## Hidden Leaf (Konoha)

Recovering from the Nine-Tails attack. Publicly stable, privately fractured — the growing rift with the Uchiha clan is the village's open secret and the richest vein for Leaf storylines. Leaf content should feel like a village performing normalcy while its foundations shift.

**Story hooks:** ANBU surveillance details, Uchiha loyalty tests, escort/rebuild missions, tension between clan elders and village command.

## Hidden Sand (Suna)

Poor in resources, rich in grievance. The Sand daimyō keeps outsourcing missions to the Leaf, starving the village of income and pride. Sand content leans on scarcity, desperation contracts, and a village willing to take jobs others would refuse.

**Story hooks:** smuggling routes through the desert, puppet-corps politics, mercenary contracts that skirt treaty lines.

## Hidden Mist (Kiri)

The Bloody Mist. Graduation is a killing exercise; bloodline users are starting to disappear. Mist content is the darkest of the three — purges brewing, defectors fleeing, and a leadership that rules by fear.

**Story hooks:** bloodline refugees, hunter-nin pursuits, defection pipelines, internal informants.

## Rogue / Missing-nin

Everyone without a village: missing-nin, Sound-aligned agents, bandits, and freelancers. Rogues can fight in village skirmishes as **mercenaries under a banner** — mechanically they score and ally for the village that hired them.

**Story hooks:** bounty boards, black-market artifact trade, double agents, work-for-hire arcs that let rogues touch every village's story.

## Non-player factions

Recurring NPC groups content authors can use (these map to the quest system's NPC target categories — see the [dev reference](../dev/objectives-and-events.md)):

| Group | Notes |
|---|---|
| Zetsu (white shadows) | Mysterious plant-like scouts appearing in the wilds; nobody knows who they serve — yet |
| Bandits | Common roadside threat; staple low-level quest target |
| Samurai | Land-of-Iron style wanderers and caravan guards |
| Iwa | Generally hostile non player village who are not in active war, but was just in a great ninja war |
